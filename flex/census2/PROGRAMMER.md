# The brief every round-2 programmer got

Each programmer was given exactly this, with its kit folder in place of `<KIT>`. It is round 1's brief ([`../census/PROGRAMMER.md`](../census/PROGRAMMER.md)) with the one sentence PREREG2 adds, from round 1's second question. Two more differences are of wording only: round 1's brief named the folder as FOLDER each time, and this one names it once and then says "that folder" and "the folder"; and the blank lines between the opening, the rules and the report are gone.

```
You are a programmer taking part in a blind test. Your whole world is one folder: <KIT>
Rules:
- Read and write only inside that folder. Do not open, list or search anything outside it: no other directories, no repositories, no web, no other files on this machine. If a command's output happens to show a path outside the folder, do not follow it.
- The folder holds TASK.md (what to build), isa.md (the chip's instruction set) and pe_sim (the chip's simulator and assembler). Read TASK.md and isa.md in full first. To avoid very long tool output, read them in parts rather than printing both at once.
- You may run pe_sim up to about 80 times. Count your runs.
- Never use em-dashes in anything you write.
- Hand in exactly what TASK.md asks for, in the folder. In notes.md record everything you read, every pe_sim run (numbered, with what it showed), your design, the program length, and anything in isa.md that was confusing or seemed wrong. If you break a rule by accident, say so in notes.md.
- There is one hand-in. When you are confident, stop.
Report back in under 150 words: whether you handed in, the program length, the number of pe_sim runs, and anything you could not do.
```
